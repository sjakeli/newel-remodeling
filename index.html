Newel Residential Remodeling

Building on twenty years of craft

Newel began as a two-person carpentry crew and grew into a full remodeling studio because homeowners kept asking for more than a fresh coat of paint — they wanted spaces that actually fit how they live. We plan every project down to the outlet placement, work in phases that keep your household running, and stay on site until the last hinge is adjusted. That attention is the whole business model.
